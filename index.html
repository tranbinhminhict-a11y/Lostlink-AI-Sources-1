How It Works

Three steps.
Zero lost items.

01

Attach & Pair

Clip LostLink to your keys, slip it into your wallet, or drop it in your bag. It pairs with your phone in under ten seconds — no setup headaches.

02

AI Learns Your Moves

LostLink silently learns where your items usually are — your desk, your car, your favorite café. It builds a personal map of your life, just like Precision Finding understands distance and direction.

When something goes missing, tap once. Your item rings, appears on the map, and your Watch nudges you in the right direction. Found, in seconds.

Our Story

We believe nothing
should stay lost.

LostLink AI was born from a simple frustration: the panic of misplacing something irreplaceable. We built the intelligence to make that feeling disappear.

The Problem

We lose more than objects.
We lose moments.

A lost passport can derail a trip. A misplaced wedding ring carries a weight no insurance can replace. Conventional trackers only tell you where something was — they don't understand your life.

Our Solution

AI that doesn't just track.
It understands.

LostLink learns your habits, your spaces, and your routines. It doesn't wait for you to ask — it proactively alerts you before something is left behind, and guides you back to it with precision.
